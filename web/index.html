Underfunded makers

Strategies whose maker's wallet balance currently falls short of their total committed amount for at least one token, across every app they've shipped to.

An attestation that doesn't show up here means nobody is currently vouching for that strategy — its heartbeat stopped refreshing, or it never existed. It does not mean the strategy closed, and it is not an error.
